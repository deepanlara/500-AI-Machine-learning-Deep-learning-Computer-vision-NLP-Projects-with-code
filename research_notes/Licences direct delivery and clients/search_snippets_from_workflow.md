  This is the official customs page. An EORI number is required before the first import from outside the EU. It is free and can only be obtained through the SOPRANO EORI online service on douane.gouv.fr. Customs warned in April 2025 about paid scam sites that offer to file it for you.   This is the official government guide for a micro-entrepreneur who imports from outside the EU. They must be registered with customs (EORI, free via SOPRANO) and file customs declarations.
